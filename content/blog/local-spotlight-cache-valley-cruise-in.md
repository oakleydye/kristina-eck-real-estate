## Local Spotlight: Cache Valley Cruise-In

Every summer, thousands of visitors make their way to Cache Valley for one of northern Utah's most beloved traditions: the Cache Valley Cruise-In.

Held annually during the first weekend of July, the Cruise-In brings together classic car enthusiasts, families, and visitors from across the country for several days filled with nostalgia, community, and some truly incredible vehicles. This year's event will be held July 2–4, 2026. What began as a local gathering has grown into one of the largest and most recognized car shows in the western United States.

The event features hundreds of classic cars, hot rods, muscle cars, trucks, and custom builds displayed throughout Logan and the surrounding communities. Visitors can enjoy the famous Main Street cruise, car shows, vendor booths, live entertainment, and the chance to meet fellow automotive enthusiasts from all walks of life.

While the cars are certainly the main attraction, the Cache Valley Cruise-In is about much more than horsepower. It's an event that brings the community together, supports local businesses, and gives visitors an opportunity to experience everything that makes Cache Valley special. Restaurants, shops, parks, and local attractions all come alive as the valley welcomes guests from near and far.

For many families, attending the Cruise-In has become a yearly tradition. Whether you're a lifelong car enthusiast or simply enjoy spending time in a vibrant community atmosphere, there's something special about seeing historic vehicles roll through the streets against the backdrop of Cache Valley's beautiful mountain scenery.

Events like the Cache Valley Cruise-In are part of what makes living in northern Utah so unique. They create lasting memories, celebrate local traditions, and showcase the strong sense of community that makes Cache Valley such a wonderful place to call home.
